# Bonsai pricing under Bread

**One company purchase. Permanent rights to covered releases. Twelve months
of new official releases.**

**Bonsai schedule P-1.0 — dated 2026-10-31; effective with the
[Bonsai adoption notice](LICENSE.md#bonsai-adoption-notice).** These are
Bonsai's prices, not universal Bread prices or a combined Bonsai/Jig license.
Email **licensing@jig.md** with Bonsai in the subject for an ordinary purchase
or a supplier-arranged customer order.

## Price

| Consolidated Group annual revenue, USD | Twelve-month purchase |
| --- | ---: |
| Below $1 million | Free business use |
| $1 million–under $10 million | $100 |
| $10 million–under $100 million | $1,000 |
| $100 million or more | $10,000 |

Count consolidated revenue across the ultimate controlling parent and its
controlled entities using [Bread's assessment rules](LICENSE.md#3-business-eligibility).
Use the last completed financial year; with no completed year, use recognized
revenue since formation without annualizing it. Funding, valuation, headcount,
and revenue attributable to Bonsai are not the measure. Declare a revenue
band; routine accounts uploads are not required. Taxes are additional and
shown in the total before purchase.

Personal non-business use and genuine evaluation are free without registration.
Ordinary product development and CI using Bonsai as an adopted tool follow the
business rules. A sole trader's business follows the same threshold.
Free and paying users receive the same software, with no seats, workflow
counts, execution fees, activation, or licensing telemetry.

## What a purchase covers

One purchase covers the named Group across its Bonsai installations and
applications. It includes existing official releases and releases first
published during the twelve-month Update Term, excluding any material outside
the Licensor's authority identified before purchase. The term includes its
start and excludes its end, using UTC.

Covered-release rights last for the duration of copyright, including permitted
independent modifications. Nonrenewal stops coverage of later official releases;
it does not cancel acquired rights. Growth does not reprice a completed
purchase. A newer uncovered release, including a security update, needs
applicable permission and may require another purchase. No particular update,
support service, or execution result is promised.

The [adoption notice](LICENSE.md#bonsai-adoption-notice) identifies the official
GitHub and npm records. Pin the full source commit of a covered official
release for the Action. A moving branch or a raw development snapshot does not
establish purchased coverage. This adoption does not publish a new release or
change the terms or contents of the earlier `v0` Action release.

The whole twelve-month price is normally paid upfront. Expressly accepted
credit does not create monthly licenses. Renewal requires agreement; cancel
any agreed automatic renewal through the purchasing channel or by email.
Future price changes follow Bread's advance-notice rule and affect only future
purchases.

## Ordinary purchasing

Send the Group and ultimate controlling parent's legal identity, billing
contact, declared revenue band, and requested start date to
**licensing@jig.md**, identifying Bonsai. The seller provides the exact terms,
price, taxes, payment instructions, and coverage record. The business purchaser
must have authority to accept the order. An ordinary purchase does not require
individual negotiation.

Permission begins when the authorized seller receives the agreed payment, or
expressly accepts an invoice order with a stated due date. The retained
confirmation identifies the Group, terms, Update Term, and official release
records. An unaccepted invoice or a supplier's own receipt is not a license.

A supplier may arrange the same purchase for its named customer with that
customer's authorization. The authorized seller confirms coverage directly to
the customer. The supplier's own license does not cover unrelated customers.

## Distribution, hosting, and other projects

Distribute Bonsai with the [license, source, and notices](LICENSES.md#distribution-and-source).
Recipients use their own applicable permission; distributors need not audit
their finances, collect fees, or report sales. There is no distribution royalty.

A provider operating its own service uses its Group's permission. A supplier
administering a customer's controlled deployment uses the customer's permission.
Generic runner or cloud-infrastructure charges do not themselves buy a Bonsai
license. Receiving independent outputs creates no software fee; generated
reports and starters also have the explicit permission in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md#additional-permission-for-generated-outputs).

The Action adapter's MIT license does not license the checker it invokes.
Jig, separately licensed dependencies, and independent applications retain
their own terms and coverage.
